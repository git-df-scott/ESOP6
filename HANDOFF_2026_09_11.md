## Hard facts established

* Two exact algebraic solutions (verified): `(7+11i)^6+(7-11i)^6+8^6+12^6+15^6 = 17^6` (unique of its shape to height 62) and
  `(9+√-249)^6+(9-√-249)^6+14^6+18^6 = 22^6` on x5 = 0 (unique to height 220). Seeds, not counterexamples.
* Slice-contact principle: coordinate forms of a Q-curve on X must be irreducible over Q (else a rational (6,1,4) point,
  forbidden by LPS). Degree 6 is the first free contact degree. Kills: Family 0, genus-0 cubic covers, the S3-symmetric
  sextic family (x5 vanishes at {0,1,∞}).
* Odd-factor principle: an odd-degree real factor of x6^6−x5^6 along a curve forces passage through [0:0:0:0:1:1].
  Kills: plane cubics u^3 = ±S (lines on Y2). (An earlier claim that it kills cubic covers V3' was a degree miscount;
  V3' has real members. Recorded in the report.)
* Every Q-conic collapses mod 7 to one coordinate (Astra). No Q-conic found; conic moduli has no rational normal form.
* Genus-1 families on X via Fano quotients: V3 (w^2 = quartic; seeds = points of Y: T^3 = x6^6−Σx_i^6), V3' (u^3 = cubic;
  seeds = points of Y2: S^2 = x6^6−Σx_i^6), Z-conics (τ-symmetric; seeds = quadratic points with a conjugate pair). All
  3-dimensional with real members; through a seed finitely many curves (10–800), defined over fields of degree > 12.
  They are correspondences, not fibrations: rational seeds lie on rational curves only for a thin set.
* Height bookkeeping: a rational curve of height H has a rational seed of height ≤ H at t = 0. Hence:
  - no rational V3' curve of height ≲ 300 (1239 Y2 seeds, all real curves enumerated, none rational);
  - no rational V3 curve of height ≲ 360 (Y has exactly two points ≤ 360; 753 and 793 real curves, none rational);
  - Z-conics through both known Z points: none rational.
* Heuristics: expected solutions ≈ 3.2e-5·log N; brute force is dead. Y(Q) ~ 1e-4·N points, Y2(Q) ~ N^2 (2924 up to 500).
* Twisted-fibre lane (Astra): per fibre c = Σa_i^6 the sextic covers E_c: X^3+cY^3 = Z^3 by a ramified (Z/2)^2 cover
  (genus 4, then 10), no Selmer shortcut; six elliptic quotients with PARI rank certificates give certified fibre rejection.
